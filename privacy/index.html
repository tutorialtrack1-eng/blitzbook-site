BlitzBook Privacy Policy

Effective 19 November 2026 · blitzbook.co.in

This policy covers the BlitzBook Android app (com.BlitzBook.aipowered on Google Play, com.vanyaliving.invoice from blitzbook.co.in) and the BlitzBook web portal at blitzbook.co.in. BlitzBook is software for invoicing, accounts, stock, payroll and reports. It is provided by BlitzBook, based in India. Questions and requests go to info@blitzbook.co.in.

In short: the books you keep in BlitzBook are yours. We keep them so that your phone and the web portal show the same data. We do not sell them, show advertisements or use analytics or tracking tools.

1. What we collect

WhatDetailsWhy

The books may contain personal information about other people, for example your customers, suppliers and employees. You enter that information and decide what is recorded; you are responsible for having the right to record it.

2. What we do not collect

  • No location, no contacts list, no photos or files other than the ones you choose to attach or import, no call or SMS logs.
  • No advertising identifiers, and no analytics, advertising or tracking software in the app or the portal.

The Android app asks for two permissions: internet access (to sync, sign in and look up bank branches), and notifications (asked the first time a stock alert is due; you can refuse).

3. Where your data is kept

  • On your device. The app keeps your books and your sign-in details in its own private storage on your phone. PDF and Excel files you create are saved to your phone's Downloads folder. If Android's own backup is switched on for your phone, Google may include the app's data in your device backup under your Google account.
  • On our server. With sync on, your account and books are stored in a database hosted by Supabase. Each account can read and change only its own data, and the companies it was given a role in. Data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • In backups. A backup of the server database is made every night and kept privately for up to 60 days, so that data can be restored after a fault.

We do not sell your data or give it to anyone for their own marketing. It reaches other companies only as far as running the service needs:

People you give a role in your company can see and change the books of that company as far as their role allows. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

5. How long we keep it

We keep your account and books for as long as your account exists. When an account is deleted, the account and its books are removed from the server, and they drop out of the backups within 60 days. Records of payments made to us are kept for as long as tax and accounting law requires.

6. Your choices and rights

  • See and correct: you can view and change your account details and your books in the app and the portal at any time.
  • Take a copy: Export / Import gives you a backup file of your books; reports export to Excel and PDF.
  • Delete: you can delete individual records yourself. To have your whole account and its data deleted, follow the steps at blitzbook.co.in/delete-account.
  • Withdraw consent or complain: write to info@blitzbook.co.in. We answer within 30 days. You also have the rights the data protection law of your country gives you, including the right to complain to its data protection authority.

7. Security

Connections to the server are encrypted, passwords are stored by the sign-in service in hashed form, and each account's data is separated from every other account's on the server. No system is perfectly secure: keep your password to yourself, give roles only to people you trust, and keep your own backups.

8. Children

BlitzBook is business software and is not meant for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

9. Changes to this policy

When this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with its date. If the change is significant we will tell you in the app or by email.
